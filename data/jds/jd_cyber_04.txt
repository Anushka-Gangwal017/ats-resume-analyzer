Job description
As a Cyber Security Intern Trainee, you will have the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the field of cybersecurity
Under the guidance of our experienced cybersecurity professionals, you will be exposed to various aspects of information security, vulnerability management, and threat detection
This internship program is designed to provide you with practical knowledge and skills that are valuable for a successful career in cybersecurity
Key Responsibilities

Security Monitoring: Assist in monitoring security alerts and incidents; participate in incident response activities to mitigate and contain security threats
Vulnerability Management: Support vulnerability assessments and penetration testing activities; help identify, prioritize, and remediate security vulnerabilities in systems and applications
Security Awareness: Contribute to security awareness initiatives, including creating and promoting security best practices among employees
Documentation: Document security processes, procedures, and guidelines; maintain up-to-date records of security configurations and assets
Security Tools: Familiarize yourself with security tools and technologies used for monitoring, detection, and prevention; assist in the evaluation and implementation of new security solutions
Collaboration: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure security measures are integrated into all aspects of the organization's operations
Research: Stay informed about the latest cybersecurity threats, trends, and technologies; conduct research to enhance the organization's security posture
Qualifications:

Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, or a related field
Basic understanding of cybersecurity concepts, including network security, encryption, authentication, and access control
Familiarity with common operating systems (Windows, Linux) and network protocols
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Ability to work independently and as part of a team
Eagerness to learn and adapt to new technologies and methodologies

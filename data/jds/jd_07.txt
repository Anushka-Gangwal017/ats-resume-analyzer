What You'll Do

Design, implement, and operate reliable, scalable, and secure production systems on AWS with strong focus on availability and performance.

Automate operational tasks using scripting (Bash/Python) and AWS-native automation (Lambda, Step Functions) to reduce toil.
Lead incident management and RCA for critical issues; drive preventive actions and implement stability improvements.
Ensure security best practices: IAM least privilege, secrets management, vulnerability remediation, and compliance (CIS benchmarks where relevant).
Collaborate with application teams on release planning, performance testing, capacity planning, and deployment strategies (blue/green, canary).
Participate in change management, release governance, and on-call rotations; maintain runbooks, SOPs, and operational documentation.

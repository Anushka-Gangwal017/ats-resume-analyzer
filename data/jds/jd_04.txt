Job Description

In this role, you will be a key driver in securing our cloud infrastructure, collaborating closely with cloud engineering, DevOps, and security teams to ensure a safe, compliant, and resilient cloud ecosystem.

 Lead cloud security incident response, including containment, investigation, and recovery
 Develop, maintain, and enhance cloud security policies, procedures, and response playbooks
 Ensure adherence to security standards and compliance frameworks including CIS Benchmarks, NIST, ISO 27001, and regulatory requirements
 Generate regular reports on vulnerability trends, risk posture, and remediation progress

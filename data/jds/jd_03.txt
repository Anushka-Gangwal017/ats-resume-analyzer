How You Will Make An Impact:

We are seeking a Cloud Engineer with expertise in Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Microsoft Azure. This position involves collaborating with various teams to maintain secure and dependable cloud environments, monitoring system performance, and proactively addressing any issues that arise.

 Key Responsibilities

Deploy and maintain cloud resources across multiple platforms, ensuring their security and efficient operation.
Configure and manage core cloud services, including virtual machines, storage solutions, databases, serverless computing, and networking components.
Leverage automation tools and command-line interfaces to streamline deployment and management tasks within the cloud environment.
Monitor cloud systems, identify and resolve issues promptly, and escalate when necessary to ensure optimal performance.
Collaborate with development, operations, and security teams to support ongoing projects and daily operations.
Stay informed about advancements and best practices in cloud technology, contributing to team discussions on potential improvements

Ideal Candidate Profile:

Demonstrated experience managing AWS and Azure cloud services and infrastructure.
Familiarity with tools and programming languages such as Terraform, Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
Strong understanding of networking, security, and cloud infrastructure concepts.
Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.

Qualifications:

A bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Technology, or related technical discipline.
Relevant AWS or Azure certifications are preferred; associate-level certifications (such as AWS Solutions Architect Associate or Microsoft Azure Administrator Associate) are highly desirable."

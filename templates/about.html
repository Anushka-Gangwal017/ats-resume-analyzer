About ResumeIQ

An AI-powered ATS resume analyzer built for Indian job seekers

The problem

A 2021 Harvard Business School study found that 88% of employers say qualified candidates are filtered out by ATS systems — not because they lack skills, but because their resume doesn't match the exact keywords the system is looking for. In India, this problem is worse because most ATS tools are trained on US job market vocabulary, not Naukri or LinkedIn India.

What makes ResumeIQ different

AI semantic matching — understands that "DSA" means "data structures", that "ML" means "machine learning". Not just keyword counting.
Skill normalization engine — 150+ mappings of informal student phrases to exact industry terms used on Naukri and LinkedIn India. This is the core research innovation of the project.
Section-level scoring — individual scores for Skills, Experience, Projects, and Summary so you know exactly which section to improve.
India JD trained — evaluated on 50 real job descriptions collected from Naukri.com and LinkedIn India across 5 domains.

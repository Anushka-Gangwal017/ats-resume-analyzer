About this project

This tool was built as an internship project under faculty guidance at MIT-ADT University, Pune.

The problem

Over 88% of employers report that qualified candidates are filtered out by ATS systems because their resume doesn't match the exact keywords in a job description — not because they lack the skills. This tool solves that.

What makes it different

  • Uses AI semantic matching (not just keywords)
  • Has a skill normalization graph trained on Indian job market vocabulary
  • Detects soft matches — understands that 'DSA' and 'data structures' mean the same thing
  • Section-level scoring for detailed feedback
